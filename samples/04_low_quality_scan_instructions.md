# Low-quality scan test
Take a phone photo of a handwritten *synthetic* note (write your own, e.g. "pt 34M, cough 3d, temp 38.1, amox 500 tid, allergy: ???"),
photograph it at an angle in dim light, and upload it. Expect `[illegible]` warnings or a clear
"too blurry to read reliably" error. A blank image should return "No readable text was found".
